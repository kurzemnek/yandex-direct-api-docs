The Feed object contains information about products and services. For more information, see [Feed management](https://yandex.com/support/direct/smart-banners/feeds.html) in the Help for Yandex.Direct.
##  [](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/en/objects/en/objects/feed#service)Servicing
Use the [Feeds](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/ref-v5/feeds/feeds.html) service for managing feeds.

[add](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/ref-v5/feeds/add.html) | [update](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/ref-v5/feeds/update.html) | [delete](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/ref-v5/feeds/delete.html) | [get](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/ref-v5/feeds/get.html)
##  [](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/en/objects/en/objects/feed#requirements)Requirements for feeds uploaded via the API
  * Acceptable size: the feed size is limited by the maximum size of the request (50 MB).
  * Valid file extensions: xml, csv, tsv, yml, xls, xlsx, gz, zip.


##  [](https://yandex.com/dev/direct/doc/en/objects/en/objects/feed#types)Business type for feed
To upload a feed, you must select the type of business that you are advertising.
**Type of business** |  **Description**  
---|---  
RETAIL |  Sales of electronics and accessories, appliances, industrial equipment, clothes, furniture, gardening products, sporting goods, construction materials, children's goods, tires and disks, cosmetics and perfumes, etc.  
HOTELS |  Hotel bookings.  
REALTY |  Real estate sales.  
AUTOMOBILES |  Sales of new and used cars.  
flights |  Sales of airline tickets.  
OTHER |  Goods and services that don't fit the other business types.
